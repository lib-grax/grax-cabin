sharpen iron with iron

a fraternity dedicated to the physical and spiritual recovery of Australian men. Founded in Woodend, Victoria.

REJECT MODERNITY, EMBRACE TRADITION

In a world of isolation and digital oppression, Woodend Men is a small group dedicated to helping men develop brotherhood in the real world through real interaction and team building. Our main aim is ensuring men are not left behind in the wake of modernity, offering a place of refuge. A brief respite.
