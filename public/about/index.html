ABOUT

In the race of digital innovation, sometimes it is important to look away from the screen and just be with the bros.

a running/cycling club (without the spandex)

Our goal is to encourage men from the greater Macedon Shire and surrounds to join us on events such as hiking, rucking, overnight camping and more, as a means to detox from the virtual world. Members will gain invaluable skills, physical strength, cardio, and friendships that will last a lifetime. We aim to give young men a chance to discuss problems, learn to overcome challenges and persevere in a world that can seem very isolating.

See the events page for details.

why woodend?

Woodend sits on the edge of the Great Dividing Range of Australia. It symbolised the start of something which is far greater than it may seem, and offers a taste of what lies ahead in the great peaks of Bogong and Kosciuszko.
